Text formatting.

Use the strong element when the text is especially important and should stand out to the reader. Browsers typically display text inside (strong) in bold, which helps draw attention to warnings, labels, or critical information.
The emphasis element (em) stresses a word or phrase within a sentence. Browsers typically display the text inside in italics, which helps highlight emphasis or meaning without interrupting the flow of text.

Important: "Important" is emphasizesed and in Bold.

Semantics

A semantic element is an HTML element whose tag name communicates the role or purpose of its content rather than simply affecting how it appears on the page. In other words, meaningful names to elements versus div soup.
